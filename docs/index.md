# 20 Days of Russian

A short, structured course for upper-intermediate to advanced learners of Russian. Each day pairs a short text with an audio passage, a focused grammar point, and a conversation exercise built directly on what you just read.

**What each day includes:**

- **Text** — a short passage, glossed and annotated
- **Audio** — a recording of the passage for listening practice
- **Grammar** — one focused point drawn from the day's text
- **Conversation** — speaking/writing prompts that build on the passage and grammar
